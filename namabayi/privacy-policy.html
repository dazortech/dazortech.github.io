1. Summary

Namaku: Baby Names & Meanings is a baby-name finder with meanings, an offline database, full filters, a name-combination generator, and favorites. The app works 100% offline for its core features. It has no account system, no sign-in, no server of its own, and no chat or community features.

The app itself does not collect, store, or send any personal data to us. Your favorite names, personal notes, and settings stay on your device. The only third-party service used by the app is Google AdMob (advertising), which processes data under its own privacy policy, as described in section 5 below.

2. Data collected by this app

Nothing leaves your device. The app does not collect, access, or send to us:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (no account is created);
  • your contacts or location;
  • your favorite names or personal notes, unless you choose to share them.

3. Data used and stored only on your device

The following data is used and stored only on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app (or when you delete it inside the app):

  • Favorite names — names and name combinations you save, along with personal notes you write for each favorite;
  • App settings — display preferences and other settings you change inside the app;
  • Built-in name database — the list of names and their meanings included in the app (10,000 names in v1).

Sharing a name card as an image only happens when you tap Share and choose a destination. The app never shares anything by itself.

4. Permissions used

The app only requests the permissions it needs:

  • Internet and network state — used only by Google AdMob to load ads. All core features (search, filters, generator, favorites) work fully offline.

The app does not request location, contacts, files/media, camera, phone, or SMS permissions. No permission is used to collect personal data for us.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners at the bottom of the Home, Name List, Favorites, Settings, and Islamic Guide screens; native ads between items in the name list; and interstitial ads only at natural breaks (at most once every 3–5 minutes, after generating a name combination or after sharing a name card). Ads never appear on the Name Detail screen, never appear when the app is opened, and never appear before showing a result.

AdMob and Google may collect and process data to serve ads, including the advertising ID and approximate location, under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google Play's terms of service. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalization, in your device Settings under Privacy → Ads.

We receive no personal data about you from AdMob, and we do not combine ad data with any other data, because the app collects nothing. There are no in-app purchases and no way to remove ads.

6. Name meanings

Name meanings in the app come from a curated database. The app does not use formulas, numerology, or any calculation to produce a meaning. If a name is not in the database, the app says so honestly ("This name is not in our database yet") and does not invent a meaning. The Guide to Choosing an Islamic Name inside the app is presented as general guidance based on referenced hadith; it is not a fatwa or official religious advice.

7. Children

Namaku: Baby Names & Meanings is an app for parents looking for a baby name — not an app for children. It has no accounts, chat, user profiles, and no way for users to contact each other. Because the app itself does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect children's personal data either. Ads are served by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

8. Data storage and deletion

Because we store no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device: uninstall the app to delete it, or delete favorites and notes one by one inside the app. Please note that name cards you have already shared are outside the app, and deleting them from the app does not delete copies you have already sent elsewhere.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes — for example, if a later version adds a feature that handles data differently. The updated policy will be posted in the Play Store listing and inside the app, with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
