1. Summary

Kalender Jawa & Weton is an Indonesian calendar app that shows the Gregorian, Hijriah, and Javanese calendars in one view, plus Javanese cultural calculators (weton, matchmaking, selamatan, good days, inauspicious days, and pranata mangsa). The app works 100% offline for its core function. It has no account system, no sign-in, no server of its own, and no chat or community features.

The app itself does not collect, store, or send any personal data to us. Your notes, reminders, history, and settings are stored on your device only. The only third-party service used by the app is Google AdMob (advertising), which processes data under its own privacy policy, as described in section 5 below.

2. Data the app itself collects

None that leaves your device. The app does not collect, access, or transmit to us:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (there is no account to create);
  • your contacts or location;
  • your notes, reminders, or calculation history, except when you choose to share or export them.

3. Data used and stored on your device only

The following is used and stored on your device only, and is deleted when you uninstall the app (or when you delete it inside the app):

  • Notes — titles and text you type for specific dates;
  • Reminders — titles, dates, times, and repeat settings (including recurring weton reminders), used only to show local notifications;
  • Calculation history — recent weton, matchmaking, selamatan, good-day, and inauspicious-day results (up to 50 entries);
  • Your weton (optional) — a birth date you enter yourself, used only to mark inauspicious days on the calendar and to pre-fill the inauspicious-day calculator;
  • App settings — theme, week start, kurup system (Asapon/Aboge), Hijriah source (Kemenag/KHGT), and notification preferences;
  • Bundled reference data — national holidays and joint leave (SKB 3 Menteri 2026–2027), weton tables, and pranata mangsa data shipped inside the app.

Sharing or exporting (a result card as an image, a note, or a backup file) only happens when you tap Share or Export and choose where to send it. The app never shares anything by itself.

4. Permissions

The app asks for only the permissions it needs:

  • Internet and network state — used only by Google AdMob to load ads. All calendar calculations work fully offline;
  • Notifications — to show reminders you created, at the time you chose (asked only when you create your first reminder, not when the app first opens);
  • Receive boot completed and exact alarms — to reschedule your reminders after the phone restarts.

The app does not request location, contacts, files/media, phone, or SMS permissions. No permission is used to collect personal data for us.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: banners at the bottom of the calendar and list screens, native ads inside lists, and interstitial ads only at natural breaks (at most once every 3–5 minutes). Ads never cover the calendar grid and never appear when the app opens.

AdMob and Google may collect and process data to show ads, including an advertising identifier and approximate location, in line with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Play services terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings under Privacy → Ads.

We do not receive personal data about you from AdMob, and we do not combine advertising data with anything else, because the app collects nothing else. There is no in-app purchase and no way to remove ads.

6. Cultural content (primbon)

Weton, matchmaking, good-day, inauspicious-day, and pranata mangsa results are presented as Javanese cultural heritage, based on commonly referenced primbon sources (including Betaljemur Adammakna). They are guidance from tradition, not certainty, and the app always says so on the result screen. Hijriah dates are calculated and may differ by ±1 day from official government determinations; public holidays in the app follow the official SKB 3 Menteri.

7. Children

Kalender Jawa & Weton is a general-audience app. It has no accounts, no chat, no user profiles, and no way for users to contact each other. Because the app itself collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either. Advertising is served by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything the app stores lives on your device: uninstall the app to remove it, or delete individual notes, reminders, or history entries inside the app. Note that results or files you have already shared or exported are outside the app, and deleting them from the app does not delete copies you have sent elsewhere.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes — for example, if a future version adds a feature that handles data differently. The updated policy will be posted on the Play Store listing and inside the app, with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
