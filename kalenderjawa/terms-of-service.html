2. What the App is

Kalender Jawa & Weton is an Indonesian calendar app. It shows the Gregorian, Hijriah, and Javanese calendars in one view, plus Javanese cultural calculators: weton, matchmaking, selamatan, good days, inauspicious days, and pranata mangsa. It works 100% offline for its core function: no account, no sign-in, no server of its own. Your notes, reminders, and history are stored on your device only. 4. Cultural results are guidance, not certainty

Weton, matchmaking, good-day, inauspicious-day, and pranata mangsa results are Javanese cultural heritage, based on commonly referenced primbon sources. They are guidance from tradition — not a prediction, not a verdict, and not a guarantee about your life, relationships, health, or fortune. Do not make important decisions (marriage, moving house, business, medical, or financial decisions) based on these results alone. The App always shows this disclaimer on every primbon result screen.

Calendar accuracy: Javanese dates follow the Sultan Agungan system (Asapon kurup by default, with an Aboge option); Hijriah dates are calculated and may differ by ±1 day from official government determinations. Public holidays follow the official SKB 3 Menteri. If a date matters for an official or religious purpose, verify it against the official source.
