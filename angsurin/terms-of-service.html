The service

Angsurin is an offline installment calculator. It lets you:

  • Simulate home mortgage (KPR), car loan, motorcycle loan, and personal loan (KTA) installments using figures you enter yourself.
  • View monthly installment amounts, payment composition charts, and full amortization schedules.
  • Save simulations on your device, compare two or three simulations side by side, and export results as PDF.

The app requires no account and works without an internet connection, except for advertising and links you choose to open.
