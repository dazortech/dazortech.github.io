Privacy Policy

Angsurin — Dazor Tech

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Overview

Angsurin ("the app", package com.dazortech.angsurin) is developed by Dazor Tech. The app is an installment calculator that helps you simulate home mortgages (KPR), car loans, motorcycle loans, and personal loans (KTA), view amortization schedules, and compare simulations.

The app is 100% offline-first: it works entirely on your device, has no user accounts and no login, and we do not operate any server that receives your data. This policy explains what the app handles and what never leaves your phone.

Data stored only on your device

  • Simulation inputs and results — the figures you type into the calculators (property or vehicle price, down payment, loan amount, interest rate, tenor) and the results they produce.
  • Simulation history — simulations you choose to save are stored in a local database (SQLite) on your device only.
  • App preferences — such as your theme choice (automatic, light, or dark).

None of this data is uploaded anywhere. You can delete saved simulations inside the app at any time, and all locally stored data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords (there are no accounts).
  • No national ID (NIK), ID card (KTP), or other identity documents.
  • No bank account numbers, card numbers, or payment information.
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • No location data — the app does not use GPS or request location permission.
  • We never sell personal data to anyone.

Internet use

All calculations are performed on your device and the app's core features work without an internet connection. The app connects to the internet only for:

  • Advertising via Google AdMob (see below).
  • Links you open yourself — for example, opening this Privacy Policy page from the app's Settings opens your browser; whatever you do in the browser is governed by that website and your browser, not by the app.
  • Files you export or share yourself — if you export a simulation as a PDF, the file is generated on your device; if you then share it through another app, that sharing is your action and is governed by the app you share with.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process data as described in Google's own policies, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) — an anonymous, resettable identifier used for advertising and ad measurement.
  • Diagnostics — app performance and crash-related data processed by Google's advertising SDK.

We do not control the data Google AdMob processes and we do not receive your Advertising ID ourselves. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data for advertising.

Data safety summary

Consistent with the app's Google Play Data safety section: Angsurin itself collects no personal data and shares no personal data. Data processed by the Google AdMob SDK (Advertising ID and diagnostics) is handled by Google as described above.

Children

Angsurin is a financial calculation tool intended for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
