Privacy Policy for Qibla Indicator - Muslim Life

Last updated: October 27, 2026

DazorTech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Qibla Indicator - Muslim Life mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Storage (Privacy-First & Offline-First)

Qibla Indicator - Muslim Life is designed with user privacy and local-first architecture at its foundation:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal identification credentials (name, email, password, phone number) to access any feature of the application.
  • 100% Local Storage: All user preferences, favorite dua bookmarks, tasbih counter tallies, and prayer notification settings are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. None of this personal app data is ever uploaded, synchronized, or transmitted to any external server.

2. Location Data (GPS & Network Coordinates)

To deliver essential Islamic worship utilities, Qibla Indicator - Muslim Life accesses your device's geographical location:

  • Sole Purpose: Location coordinates (latitude, longitude, and elevation) are used strictly to calculate the accurate compass bearing towards the Holy Kaaba in Mecca and to calculate precise daily Islamic prayer times (Fajr/Subuh, Dhuhr/Dzuhur, Asr/Ashar, Maghrib, and Isha/Isya).
  • Strictly On-Device Calculation: All astronomical equations and Qibla azimuth formulas are executed directly on your physical device. Your coordinates are never sent across the internet, never collected into user tracking profiles, and never saved on external servers.
  • User Control: Location permission is optional; you can enable or revoke location access at any time through your device's operating system settings.

3. Device Sensors (Magnetometer & Accelerometer)

The interactive Qibla compass feature requires access to your device's built-in hardware sensors:

  • The app accesses the magnetometer (geomagnetic field sensor) and accelerometer in real time to calculate the device orientation relative to Magnetic and True North.
  • Sensor data is processed dynamically in memory for visual rendering on your screen and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted outside your device.

4. Audio & Notification Reminders

The application provides automated Adzan reminders and prayer notifications:

  • All Adzan audio files are bundled locally inside the application package and played locally through Android system notifications and audio managers.
  • The app does not access your microphone, call logs, or audio recordings.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements that support the ongoing maintenance and free availability of the app. Google AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable diagnostic information in accordance with their privacy policies, including:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Coarse location derived from IP address for regional ad relevance
  • App performance, stability, and crash diagnostic metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your physical device, you have complete and total control over your data. You can clear your tasbih counter, remove favorite duas, or reset all preferences directly within the app settings. Furthermore, uninstalling the app or clearing the app data via Android Settings permanently purges all stored information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
