Privacy Policy for SplitYuk

Last updated: October 26, 2026

DazorTech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SplitYuk mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Storage (Privacy-First & Offline-First)

SplitYuk is built with user privacy and local-first architecture at its core:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to use SplitYuk.
  • 100% Local Storage: All your bills, item breakdowns, participant names, calculation history, and split records are stored locally on your device via SQLite. None of this data is ever uploaded or synced to external servers.

2. Camera and Receipt OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

SplitYuk provides a smart receipt-scanning feature:

  • On-Device Machine Learning: Receipt OCR processing is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit.
  • Zero Cloud Transmission: Receipt photos taken with the camera or selected from your gallery are never sent over the internet or uploaded to any third-party server.

3. Payment Accounts & QRIS Images

To help you share settlement instructions with friends, SplitYuk allows you to store bank/e-wallet accounts and upload static QRIS images:

  • All payment numbers, account holder names, and QRIS photo files are stored exclusively in your device's private app storage.
  • We do not collect, process, or transmit your banking credentials or QRIS pictures to external servers. They are solely displayed within the app and shared when you explicitly choose to share bill summaries.

4. Contacts Access (Optional)

SplitYuk includes an optional "Pick from Contacts" feature:

  • This permission is only requested when you voluntarily choose to populate a participant's name and WhatsApp phone number from your address book.
  • SplitYuk never collects, uploads, synchronizes, or shares your contacts with any third party.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements that support the free maintenance of the app. Google AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable diagnostic information in accordance with their privacy policies, including:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location derived from IP address
  • App performance and crash diagnostic metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

Since all data resides on your physical device, you have complete control over your data. You may edit or delete any bill, participant record, payment account, or QRIS image directly within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings will permanently erase all stored information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
