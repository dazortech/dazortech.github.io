4. Balance Accuracy Disclaimer

The balance and transaction history displayed are the values read from your card's chip at the moment of scanning:

  • Pending updates: A top-up made through mobile banking, an e-wallet, or an e-commerce platform may not appear on the card until the card balance is updated through the issuer's official channel (for example by tapping the card at an official app, ATM, or merchant terminal). In that case the app will show the balance currently stored on the card, which is correct as stored.
  • Hardware variance: Successful reading depends on your device having a working NFC sensor and on the card being held correctly against it. Thick phone cases, metal objects, and weak or damaged card chips can cause read failures.
  • Disputes: If you believe a balance or transaction shown by your card is wrong, only the issuing bank can investigate and correct it. Please contact the issuing bank; we cannot adjust card balances or reverse transactions.
