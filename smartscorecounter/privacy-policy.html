Privacy Policy for Smart Score Counter

Last updated: October 11, 2026

DazorTech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Smart Score Counter mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Smart Score Counter is designed with user privacy in mind:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register or provide any personal information (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the app.
  • Local Storage Only: All match data, player names, custom presets, and game histories are stored locally on your device using on-device encrypted storage. None of this data is ever transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements that support the free operation of the app. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information in accordance with their privacy policies, including:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location derived from IP address
  • App performance and crash diagnostic metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. Device Permissions

Smart Score Counter only requests minimum necessary permissions:

  • Internet Access: Exclusively for fetching Google AdMob advertisements and sharing match results.
  • Vibration / Haptic: For tactile button press feedback.
  • Audio Playback: For whistle, buzzer, and scoring sound effects.

We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
